How this game is made

The developer directs

One person. He chose the game and its shape: plan in the hut, walk to the lighthouse, defend from it. He plays the build at every gate, judges the pictures, and says no when something is not good enough. What he asked for and what he thought is retold here in the agent's words.

AI coding agents build

Claude Code writes all of the game's code (C++ in Unreal Engine 5.9), drives the engine's editor through scripts, and runs every tool below. On Friday several agents work at once, each in its own copy of the code and owning its own files: the hut and the walk, the map and the plan, the lighthouse's stations, the tablet and the guide, the battle and its economy, the look and the storm, explosions, assets and sound, photoreal surfaces, the HUD, a lane that keeps refining whatever looks least real, a verifier that plays the game and hunts for faults, and this log. One of them merges their work and keeps the game building.

One PC, one exception

Pictures, voices, music, sounds and effects are made on one PC with one graphics card, and so are the small props' 3D models. The other 3D models are the exception: Rodin makes them in the cloud, always from one of our own pictures.

From outside: what was not made here

  • Unreal Engine's own content the Water plugin's ocean, sky, cloud and fog, basic shapes, an engine noise texture, the glTF importer's base material, the engine's fonts. engine licence
  • NVIDIA DLSS and Reflex the upscaling plugin and the engine's low-latency plugin, named in words only (no logos). NVIDIA's licence
  • This site's fonts Big Shoulders Display and Source Serif 4, not in the game. SIL Open Font Licence

Made before the jam

The engine build, the AI tools and their settings, the build and packaging scripts, this site's generator, and FYR's first design and prompt library on paper (written on the evening of 11 November, after the theme was announced) were prepared before the jam began, as the rules allow, and are declared with the entry. The lighthouse defence itself is the developer's own idea from before the jam. No game content (no code of the game, picture, model, sound or level) was made before Thursday 12 November, 06:00 Danish time.

FYR is a solo entry in the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam (theme: Director's Cut), made between Thursday 12 November and Sunday 15 November 2026, Danish time. Team name: Faluf.

This site is built from plain text entries by a small script and holds no tracking, no cookies and no requests to other servers. The game's source is in a private repository; its commit history shows when each thing was made.
