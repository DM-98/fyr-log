What went wrong 19

A glass box shows the cracks too. Every failure the log tells, newest first: what broke, in a few lines, and a link to where it is told in full with what was done about it.

  1. Sat 14 Nov, 20:35 Four faults that every automated check had passed

    Every run of the game is checked by its log, its tests and strips of pictures taken every few seconds. None of that saw these four faults; looking at the video's takes frame by frame did. The shots they spoiled were left out of the video.

    Told in The gameplay video, and the faults its takes caught

  2. Sat 14 Nov, 20:35 Three of the first fixes were wrong guesses, and three more were too weak

    The fourth night was recorded seven more times between 19:30 and 20:25, each time with the fixes built so far. The first round was wrong three times. The white disc had been put down to the great lens's fading power after a switch to the gun; the new take showed the disc starting the moment the mothership's shield went down, seconds after any switch.

    Told in The gameplay video, and the faults its takes caught

  3. Sat 14 Nov, 14:35 Our checks passed an ending in which the keeper walked through solid stone

    The ending had passed every automated run and every strip of pictures, and the developer found the fault in the first video he saw. The walk home put the keeper's feet on the ground by a trace straight down that ignores the hut (whose meshes have no collision), so on the doorstep his feet stood on the rock underneath it.

    Told in Watching the ending, and a keeper who moves like a tired old man

  4. Sat 14 Nov, 14:35 The motion model could not use the graphics card, and its first batch was stopped halfway

    With the bundled PyTorch every take ran on the processor, and a whole batch of takes ran inside one step of the editor's test. The headless editor's watchdog ends a run that shows no sign of life for 15 minutes, and it stopped the batch partway through the third of its four clips.

    Told in Watching the ending, and a keeper who moves like a tired old man

  5. Sat 14 Nov, 01:25 Every lightning strike lit the inside of the hut, the planning phase included

    In the strips of the ending the hut's wall flashed for a single frame now and then. The cause is the sky: its lightning light sits on a lighting channel that was meant to reach nothing on the ground, but the hut's room uses the same channel, so every strike lights the room through its roof.

    Told in The first ending: a drone films the keeper, and he watches it at home

  6. Fri 13 Nov, 23:30 The balance misses its own target on Easy, and the first bot played like nobody would

    The lane's own target for Easy is a lamp between 55 and 85 percent at the end of a night. It is met on Nights 2 and 4, not reliably on 1 and 3. Night 1 stays at 94 to 100 percent for anyone who fires, because the new harvester dies on its way in.

    Told in Friday night: the first full package, half an hour of play, and a night of fixes

  7. Fri 13 Nov, 23:30 The game's drive ran out of space in the middle of the night

    At 00:07 the drive that holds FYR had 0 GB free. The last two gigabytes went to a fresh copy of the whole project that the ending's lane had started for a long test run.6 GB: still too little for a package or several builds.

    Told in Friday night: the first full package, half an hour of play, and a night of fixes

  8. Fri 13 Nov, 20:03 The rebuild cut the developer's 64 achievements to 30, and nobody asked him

    When FYR was rebuilt on Friday, the new design cut the game's achievements from 64 to 30 honours and 2 hidden ones, none with a reward, and no one asked the developer first. He had asked on the first day for more than fifty, some of them hidden, each with a reward, and the first version had 64 with 8 hidden.

    Told in Friday evening: the first playable, the developer's notes as they came, and two things we got wrong

  9. Fri 13 Nov, 20:03 A note from the first day sat in a file for a day and a half

    On Thursday the developer asked that the title not carry a line explaining what the name means. The note was written down that day and then never done, through the first version and the whole rebuild, until he saw the line on the title again at 17:57 and asked a second time.

    Told in Friday evening: the first playable, the developer's notes as they came, and two things we got wrong

  10. Fri 13 Nov, 20:03 The package grew past one gigabyte, and two of our guesses about why were wrong

    The first playable's zip was 1,245 MB, over the 1 GB that a browser upload of the entry takes. The integrator's first two ideas were wrong: a cap on normal maps that touched nothing (every model texture sits in one group), and a claim that the package was stored without compression, which the verifier had already shown it was not.

    Told in Friday evening: the first playable, the developer's notes as they came, and two things we got wrong

  11. Fri 13 Nov, 17:44 The first playable's package failed

    The package for the developer's first play was started at 17:16 and failed in the cook at 17:20: with the two editor plugins made editor-only, the cook still found their content and could not cook it. A second attempt at 17:23, which told the cook to leave that content out, tripped a check of its own.

    Told in Catching up, part three: the developer's first look and what each lane did, the first package, the newest tools, and a stop at 17:30

  12. Fri 13 Nov, 17:44 Every agent stopped at once, and this log fell four hours behind

    At about 17:30 every agent working on FYR stopped at the same moment: the week's allowance of the model they ran on had run out. Nothing was lost, because each lane keeps its notes, its branch and its board current; the lanes were restarted from them on another model and carried on.

    Told in Catching up, part three: the developer's first look and what each lane did, the first package, the newest tools, and a stop at 17:30

  13. Fri 13 Nov, 17:43 The first explosions looked like cotton

    The first simulations failed in four ways. Smoke and fire came out as clusters of round balls, because the fuel was laid down in a few large blobs. One plume was cut flat where it hit the top of its simulation box. A fireball's fire and smoke split along a straight line.

    Told in Catching up, part two: rain on the graphics card, latency measured for real, photoreal rock, and explosions from Blender

  14. Fri 13 Nov, 17:41 Rodin's lens, and gloved hands it could not make

    The lens. Rodin's great lens came back with soft, melted-looking rings (a second take with Rodin's de-lighting read as plastic). The developer turned the set down at 13:50 and named the lens as the reason. Twelve minutes later the assets lane had built it in code instead, in Blender: eight bullseye panels, rings of real glass prisms, a brass frame and two handles, 29,000 faces.

    Told in Catching up, part one: the hut from Rodin, a lens sent back, the tablet, and the rules of the night

  15. Fri 13 Nov, 17:41 Three rigs before the keeper could walk

    For one outside shot on the way to the tower, Rodin rigged the keeper made on Thursday. The first rigging job failed outright: the walk asked for a hips bone the chosen skeleton did not have. The second worked, but the coat ballooned wherever a hand passed it.

    Told in Catching up, part one: the hut from Rodin, a lens sent back, the tablet, and the rules of the night

  16. Fri 13 Nov, 13:32 Every lane's build broke every other lane's game

    Each lane has its own copy of the game's code, but they all share one engine, built from source. At 13:22 the integrator found that a build in any worktree quietly relinked one of the engine's modules, and the engine's build tool then gave the engine a new build id.

    Told in The skeleton runs: hut, walk, night, held, hut; and the first honest look at ray tracing

  17. Thu 12 Nov, 15:40 The editor waited for a click nobody could make

    At 06:04 the editor started and then did nothing for seven minutes. The preview engine's own project template asks for one version of the build settings and the preview editor wants the next, so it showed a "Target Upgrade Required" box with Yes and No and waited for a person.

    Told in Day one: a gun on a lighthouse in a storm

  18. Thu 12 Nov, 15:40 A zip that held nothing

    The first packaged build was asked for by Thursday 18:00 and existed at 07:38, but only on the third try, and both failures were the agent's own. The first time, it wrote new menu code while the release build was compiling, and the build picked up the half-tested files.

    Told in Day one: a gun on a lighthouse in a storm

  19. Thu 12 Nov, 15:40 The wrong game, for five hours

    From 06:00 to about 11:20 the work produced a polished gallery shooter with six scripted scenes and an ending: the film idea had been built as a short story with a gun in it. What the developer wanted was a game you keep playing.

    Told in Day one: a gun on a lighthouse in a storm
