The developer's note

I should be honest about one thing this log does not dwell on.

On Thursday afternoon, the first day of the jam, I stopped working on FYR. I had played it and it felt like a shooting gallery with no soul, so I threw the plan away and started a different game: a first-person horror game, GLEMT. For most of a day, through Thursday night and into Friday morning, that was my entry.

Then I played it, and it bored me. I do not play horror games. I play competitive shooters, where the aim has to be precise and every hit has to feel right, and I was trying to make something I would never pick up myself. That is a bad way to make a game.

So on Friday I went back to FYR. It is the same game, revised: I kept what I liked about it, the gun, the sound and the storm, and rebuilt it around the idea I should have had on day one: plan the night at a monitor in the keeper's hut, walk out into the storm, and fight from the tower. None of the horror game is in FYR; what I brought back was a few tools, and a much clearer idea of what I actually enjoy.

It cost me almost a day of a four-day jam. I do not regret trying it, and I am glad I did not give up on the game I started. FYR is the one I wanted to finish and did finish at the end.

If you want to see what that day went into, its production log is here, as it stood: https://dm-98.github.io/fyr-log/glemt/index.html

The developer of FYR

FYR is a solo entry in the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam (theme: Director's Cut), made between Thursday 12 November and Sunday 15 November 2026, Danish time. Team name: Faluf.

This site is built from plain text entries by a small script and holds no tracking, no cookies and no requests to other servers. The game's source is in a private repository; its commit history shows when each thing was made.
